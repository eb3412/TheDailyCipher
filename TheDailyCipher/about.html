ABOUT THE PROJECT

Cryptography should be interactive.

The Daily Cipher is being built as a free platform for learning, practicing, and competing in cryptography.

Our Goal

Make cryptography easier to learn by combining education, daily practice, interactive visualizations, and competition in one platform.

Who Is It For?

Students, puzzle solvers, cryptography enthusiasts, Science Olympiad competitors, teachers, and anyone interested in codebreaking.
