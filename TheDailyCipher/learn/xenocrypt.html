SPANISH SUBSTITUTION • CODEBUSTERS

Spanish Xenocrypt

Apply monoalphabetic substitution techniques to Spanish plaintext, including Ñ.

OVERVIEW

What it is

This format is included as a Science Olympiad Codebusters practice mode on The Daily Cipher.

The practice generator varies keys, plaintext, and cribs so repeated attempts train the method rather than one memorized example.

A Spanish monoalphabetic cryptogram using a K1 alphabet constructed from an English keyword.
MECHANICS

How it works

Construct the requested keyed alphabet by removing repeated letters from the keyword and appending unused alphabet letters.

Apply one fixed substitution throughout the message; Patristocrats remove spaces, while Xenocrypt plaintext is Spanish.
